## 🧾 What I do for clients

### Tax advisory

| Service | What you get |
|---|---|
| **Direct tax advisory** | Corporate income tax and Zakat: computations, returns and planning, prepared from your books |
| **Withholding tax (WHT) advisory** | Which payments to non-residents need WHT, at what rate, and when a tax treaty lowers it |
| **International tax** | Advice on cross-border deals and structures: double tax treaties, permanent establishment risk and foreign income |
| **Saudi VAT** | VAT returns and reviews, with records kept ready for ZATCA |

### Accounting

| Service | What you get |
|---|---|
| **ERPNext setup** | Your own accounting system: chart of accounts, VAT settings, opening balances and user access |
| **Monthly bookkeeping & close** | Reconciled bank and ledgers, plus a trial balance, P&L and balance sheet every month |
| **Group consolidation** | Standalone and consolidated statements for groups with several companies and currencies (IFRS / SOCPA) |
| **Year-end & audit support** | Closed periods, a full audit trail and schedules ready for your auditor |

## 🔒 How your books stay safe

- **One system per client.** Your data never shares a database with another client's.
- **Closed months stay closed.** Once a period is locked, nobody can quietly change it.
- **Every correction leaves a trail.** Entries are cancelled and amended, never deleted.
- **Kept in the Kingdom.** Your books can run on a server inside Saudi Arabia.
